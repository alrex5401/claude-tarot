隱私政策

最後更新：2026 年 5 月 23 日　・　生效日：2026 年 5 月 23 日

一句話總結：本 App 不收集任何個人資料；抽牌紀錄、心得、API 金鑰、提醒設定全部僅存於您的裝置本機，不上傳任何伺服器。

1. 我們是誰

TAROT（以下簡稱「本 App」）是一款 78 張塔羅抽牌與 AI 解讀工具，所有功能皆在您的裝置本機運作，沒有後端伺服器。

2. 我們不收集的資料

本 App 不收集、不儲存、不上傳以下任何資料至我方伺服器（事實上我方並無伺服器）：

  • 姓名、Email、電話、Apple ID、IDFA / IDFV 等任何個人識別資訊
  • 地理位置、裝置位置權限
  • 通訊錄、相簿、麥克風、相機、健康資料等系統權限
  • 使用行為分析（無 Google Analytics、無 Firebase、無 Sentry、無 Crashlytics）
  • 廣告追蹤識別碼（無任何廣告 SDK）

3. 僅儲存在您裝置本機的資料

以下資料皆透過 iOS 系統 API（Capacitor Preferences、iOS Keychain）儲存於您的裝置本機，您解除安裝 App 即完全刪除：

  • 抽牌紀錄：每次抽牌的時間、牌組、正逆位、模式、您自行填寫的問題與心得
  • OpenAI API 金鑰（若您主動於設定頁貼上）：以 iOS Keychain 加密儲存，本 App 程式碼以外無法讀取
  • 每日提醒設定：開關狀態與提醒時間
  • 付費解鎖狀態：透過 Apple StoreKit 驗證，狀態僅存本機

4. 主動觸發的第三方傳輸

4-1 OpenAI（AI 解讀功能，可選）

當您在抽牌結果頁主動點擊「✨ AI 解讀」按鈕時，本 App 會將以下內容直接從您的裝置傳送至 OpenAI API（不經過任何中間伺服器）：

  • 抽到的牌組（牌名、正逆位、牌位）
  • 您自行填寫的問題（若有）
  • 您於設定頁貼上的 OpenAI API 金鑰（透過 HTTPS 標頭認證）

此資料的後續處理依 OpenAI 的隱私政策辦理，本 App 無法控制：openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/

若您不希望任何資料離開您的裝置，請勿於設定頁貼上 OpenAI API 金鑰；不貼上即無法觸發 AI 解讀功能，其他功能（抽牌、紀錄、提醒、Widget）皆不受影響。

4-2 Apple StoreKit（付費解鎖）

付費解鎖功能透過 Apple 內建的 StoreKit 進行，本 App 僅取得「是否已購買」的布林結果，不取得您的 Apple ID、付款方式或交易明細。完整付款資料由 Apple 處理，依 Apple 隱私政策辦理：apple.com/legal/privacy/

5. 兒童隱私

本 App 不針對未滿 13 歲（或您所在司法管轄區之相應年齡）兒童設計，亦不蓄意收集兒童個人資料。本 App 提供之塔羅內容屬靈性 / 心理探索性質，建議成年人使用。

6. 您的權利

  • 刪除單筆抽牌紀錄：於紀錄頁點各紀錄旁的刪除按鈕
  • 清除 OpenAI 金鑰：於設定頁點「清除金鑰」按鈕
  • 關閉每日提醒：於設定頁切換提醒開關
  • 刪除全部資料：從 iOS 主畫面長按 App 圖示 → 刪除 App，所有本機資料隨之清除

7. 資料安全

OpenAI API 金鑰透過 @aparajita/capacitor-secure-storage 寫入 iOS Keychain，由 iOS 系統以裝置硬體加密保護。其他資料透過 Capacitor Preferences 寫入 iOS 沙盒，僅本 App 可讀取。

所有與 OpenAI API 的通訊皆透過 HTTPS / TLS 1.2+ 加密。

8. 內容免責聲明

塔羅解讀屬靈性 / 心理探索工具，僅供自我覺察與反思參考，不構成醫療、心理治療、法律、財務、投資或任何專業建議。若您正面臨情緒困擾或心理危機，請尋求專業協助（台灣 24 小時生命線：1995；張老師專線：1980；衛福部安心專線：1925）。

9. 政策變更

本政策若有重大變更，將更新本頁面之「最後更新」日期；變更內容自更新日起生效。建議您定期回顧本頁面。

10. 聯絡方式

ENGLISH VERSION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 23, 2026　・　Effective date: May 23, 2026

TL;DR: This app collects no personal data. All draw history, notes, API keys, and reminder settings are stored locally on your device only. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

1. Who We Are

TAROT is a 78-card tarot drawing and AI-reading utility. All functionality runs locally on your device. There is no backend server.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

The app does not collect, store, or upload any of the following to our servers (we have no servers):

  • Name, email, phone number, Apple ID, IDFA / IDFV, or any personal identifiers
  • Location data or location permissions
  • Contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or health data
  • Usage analytics (no Google Analytics, Firebase, Sentry, or Crashlytics)
  • Advertising identifiers (no ad SDKs whatsoever)

3. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored on your device only (via Capacitor Preferences and iOS Keychain) and is fully deleted when you uninstall the app:

  • Draw history: timestamp, cards, orientation, mode, your typed questions and notes
  • OpenAI API key (if you choose to enter one): encrypted in iOS Keychain
  • Daily reminder settings: on/off state and reminder time
  • Premium unlock status: verified via Apple StoreKit, stored locally

4. User-Triggered Third-Party Transmissions

4-1 OpenAI (AI Reading Feature, Optional)

When you actively tap the "✨ AI Reading" button on a draw result, the app sends the following directly from your device to the OpenAI API (no intermediary server):

  • The drawn cards (name, orientation, position)
  • Your typed question, if any
  • Your OpenAI API key (via HTTPS Authorization header)

Subsequent handling of this data is governed by OpenAI's privacy policy, beyond our control: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/

If you prefer no data ever leaves your device, simply do not enter an OpenAI API key. All other features (drawing, history, reminders, Widget) work without it.

4-2 Apple StoreKit (Premium Unlock)

Premium unlock is handled by Apple's built-in StoreKit. The app receives only a boolean "purchased" result and never accesses your Apple ID, payment method, or transaction details. Payment data is handled by Apple per Apple's privacy policy: apple.com/legal/privacy/

5. Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction) and does not knowingly collect data from children. Tarot content is spiritual / introspective in nature and intended for adult use.

6. Your Rights

  • Delete a single draw record: tap the delete button next to any record on the history page
  • Clear OpenAI key: tap "Clear Key" on the settings page
  • Disable daily reminder: toggle the reminder switch on the settings page
  • Delete all data: long-press the app icon on iOS home screen → Delete App, removing all local data

7. Data Security

OpenAI API keys are stored via @aparajita/capacitor-secure-storage in iOS Keychain, protected by iOS hardware-backed encryption. Other data is stored via Capacitor Preferences in the iOS app sandbox, readable only by this app.

All communication with the OpenAI API uses HTTPS / TLS 1.2+.

8. Content Disclaimer

Tarot readings are spiritual / introspective tools for self-reflection and do not constitute medical, psychological, legal, financial, investment, or any other professional advice. If you are experiencing emotional distress or a mental health crisis, please seek professional support.

9. Policy Changes

If this policy materially changes, the "Last updated" date above will be revised; changes take effect from the updated date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
