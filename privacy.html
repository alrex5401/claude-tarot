隱私政策

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 25 日　・　生效日：2026 年 10 月 25 日

一句話總結：我們不收集你的個人資料。塔羅密語的 iPhone App 沒有伺服器，抽牌紀錄和心得存在你的 iPhone，並自動備份到你自己的 iCloud；只有在你按下按鈕、而且同意之後，內容才會送到 OpenAI 或 ChatGPT。網站不存任何紀錄。連接器（Tarot Whisper）的工具只要求抽牌需要的參數，不要求也不保存你的問題；透過 ChatGPT 呼叫時，OpenAI 會另外附帶大概位置等資料（詳見第 12 節）。

1. 我們是誰

本政策涵蓋「塔羅密語」（Tarot Whisper）的三個產品，都由 Alex Yeh 製作：

  • 塔羅密語 iPhone App（英文系統顯示為 TAROT，以下簡稱「本 App」）：一本塔羅日記，抽牌、寫下當下的想法，之後回頭看自己的心情起伏。本 App 沒有後端伺服器，也沒有帳號系統。第 2～10 節說的是本 App。
  • 塔羅密語網站：tarot.angelz13.com，在瀏覽器裡抽牌、產生可以帶去問 AI 的提示文字。見第 11 節。
  • 塔羅密語連接器（Tarot Whisper）：MCP 連接器（網址 https://tarot.angelz13.com/mcp），加到你自己的 Claude 或 ChatGPT 之後，在對話裡抽牌、查牌義。見第 12 節與連接器說明頁。

2. 我們不收集的資料

本 App 沒有伺服器，所以我們不會收到、也不會保存你在本 App 裡的任何資料，包括：

  • 姓名、Email、電話、Apple ID、IDFA／IDFV 等任何可以識別你的資訊
  • 位置、通訊錄、相簿、麥克風、相機、健康資料（本 App 不要求這些權限）
  • 使用行為分析（沒有 Google Analytics、Firebase、Sentry、Crashlytics 這類分析或當機回報套件）
  • 廣告識別碼（沒有任何廣告套件）

3. 存在你 iPhone 上的資料

以下資料透過 iOS 的 Capacitor Preferences 與 Keychain 存在你的手機，只有本 App 讀得到：

  • 抽牌紀錄：時間、抽到的牌、正逆位、牌位、模式，以及你填的問題、心得、心情、主題標籤；按過 AI 解讀的，解讀內容也會一起存。
  • 自訂的主題標籤
  • OpenAI API 金鑰（只有你在設定頁貼上才有）：存在 iOS Keychain，不會放進本 App 的 iCloud 備份。
  • 設定：每日提醒、解讀引擎與模型，以及你對第三方 AI 的同意紀錄。
  • 主畫面小工具：今天的牌透過 iOS 的 App Group 跟小工具共用，只在這支手機上。

4. 你自己的 iCloud 備份

抽牌紀錄和自訂標籤有變動時，本 App 會自動存一份到你自己的 iCloud（本 App 專屬的資料夾，最多 10 分鐘一份；清空紀錄前也會另外存一份）。備份放在你的 Apple 帳號裡，由 Apple 依 Apple 隱私政策保管，我們看不到、也拿不到。

要救回紀錄，到「紀錄」頁按「從 iCloud 還原」挑一個時間點。要一次刪光所有備份，到 iPhone「設定 > Apple 帳號 > iCloud > 管理儲存空間」找到本 App 刪除。

5. iPhone 本機 AI

預設的 AI 解讀用 Apple 的 Foundation Models 框架，直接在你的 iPhone 上產生，你的問題、牌和紀錄不會離開手機。這需要支援 Apple 智慧的 iPhone，並開啟 Apple 智慧。

6. 你同意之後才會送出的資料

下面兩個去處，第一次用到時本 App 會先說明會送出什麼、問你同不同意；你同意之後才會送出，之後可以在「設定 › 第三方 AI 分享」逐一撤回。撤回只影響之後的使用；已經送出的內容由對方依它的隱私政策處理，要刪除請向對方申請。

6-1 OpenAI（AI 解讀用 OpenAI 時）

你在設定頁選了 OpenAI，或本機 AI 不能用而你有設金鑰時，本 App 會用你自己的 API 金鑰、直接從你的手機連到 OpenAI API（不經過任何中間伺服器），送出：

  • 這次的問題、抽到的牌和牌位（多擇一牌陣會含你填的選項名稱）
  • 之後的追問，以及從本機 AI 轉過來時的整段對話
  • 在「紀錄」頁用回顧時，那段期間的抽牌紀錄和心得

OpenAI 依它的隱私政策處理這些內容：openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/

6-2 ChatGPT 網頁

按「一鍵問 ChatGPT」或「詢問 ChatGPT」時，本 App 會開啟 ChatGPT 網頁，並把這次的問題、抽到的牌和牌位帶過去；從解讀或對話中途過去時，會帶上整段對話（你的追問和 AI 的回答）。ChatGPT 由 OpenAI 經營，之後在那邊的使用依 OpenAI 的隱私政策處理；本 App 不會另外保存。

不想讓任何內容離開手機的話，只用本機 AI、不設 OpenAI 金鑰、不按 ChatGPT 的按鈕就可以；抽牌、紀錄、回顧、提醒、小工具都不受影響。

7. Siri、捷徑、「日誌」與提醒

  • 用 Siri 抽牌：在你的手機上執行，抽到的牌存進本機紀錄。
  • 送到「日誌」：本 App 把那筆紀錄和牌陣圖暫時放進剪貼簿，交給你自己安裝的捷徑存進 Apple 的「日誌」App，之後清掉剪貼簿。資料留在你的手機和 Apple 的服務裡，依 Apple 隱私政策處理。
  • 每日提醒：由手機本機排定的通知，不經過任何推播伺服器。

8. 兒童隱私

本 App 不是為未滿 13 歲（或你所在地區規定的相應年齡）的兒童設計，也不會蓄意收集兒童的個人資料。塔羅內容屬於靈性與自我探索，建議成年人使用。

9. 你可以怎麼管理自己的資料

  • 刪除單筆紀錄：在「紀錄」頁按那一筆的「刪除」
  • 清除 OpenAI 金鑰：在設定頁 OpenAI API Key 那一區按「清除」
  • 撤回第三方 AI 同意：在「設定 › 第三方 AI 分享」逐一撤回
  • 關閉每日提醒：在設定頁關掉提醒開關
  • 刪除 iCloud 備份：照第 4 節的步驟
  • 刪除手機上的全部資料：在主畫面長按 App 圖示 → 刪除 App。注意：iCloud 備份不會跟著刪，要照第 4 節另外刪。

10. 資料安全

OpenAI API 金鑰透過 @aparajita/capacitor-secure-storage 存進 iOS Keychain，由 iOS 以裝置硬體加密保護。其他資料存在 iOS 分給本 App 的沙盒裡，只有本 App 讀得到。跟 OpenAI API 的連線全程走 HTTPS 加密。

11. 網站「塔羅密語」

  • 不收集、不存檔：網站沒有帳號，我們不設 Cookie，也沒有加任何分析或追蹤程式（Cloudflare 的機器人防護可能設定它自己的安全 Cookie __cf_bm，見下面「字型與主機」）。抽牌在你的瀏覽器裡完成，你的問題和抽到的牌不會存下來、也不會傳給我們；重新整理或關掉頁面就沒有了。
  • 複製 Prompt：內容只放進你裝置的剪貼簿。
  • 詢問 ChatGPT／詢問 Claude：按下按鈕會開新分頁，把這次的問題、抽到的牌和牌位放在網址裡，帶到 ChatGPT（OpenAI 經營）或 Claude（Anthropic 經營）網頁；這兩個按鈕不會另外跳出確認視窗。之後在那邊的使用，依 OpenAI 或 Anthropic 的隱私政策處理。
  • 字型與主機：網站字型從 Google Fonts 載入、網站由 Cloudflare 託管，你的瀏覽器連線時會送出一般的連線資訊（例如 IP 位址），依 Google 與 Cloudflare 的隱私政策處理。

12. 塔羅密語連接器（Tarot Whisper）

你在自己的 Claude 或 ChatGPT 加了這個連接器之後，你的 AI 決定要抽牌或查牌義時，會連到我們放在 Cloudflare 上的程式。解讀由你自己的 AI 完成，你和 AI 的對話留在你自己的 Claude 或 ChatGPT 帳號裡。連接器沒有帳號，程式不設 Cookie，也沒有分析或追蹤程式；Cloudflare 的機器人防護可能設定它自己的安全 Cookie（__cf_bm），依 Cloudflare 隱私政策處理。

12-1 收到的資料

  • 工具參數：抽牌模式（單張、三張、每日一張）和要不要有逆位；或你要查的牌名與正逆位。工具沒有可以放問題文字的欄位，多出來的欄位會被拒絕。
  • 協定資訊：MCP 協定版本、要呼叫哪個工具這類技術欄位。
  • ChatGPT 附帶的資料：透過 ChatGPT 呼叫時，OpenAI 會在每次呼叫附上大概位置（城市、地區、國家、時區、經緯度）、語系、用戶端資訊、匿名使用者 ID，以及對話與組織的識別碼。這些由 OpenAI 決定要不要送，我們無法關掉。
  • 連線資訊：連接器由 Cloudflare 託管，連線時會經過 Cloudflare，帶有 IP 位址等一般連線資訊。Claude 的呼叫是從 Anthropic 的伺服器發出。

12-2 用途

只用來抽牌、回傳牌義，以及限制短時間內的呼叫次數、防止濫用：ChatGPT 附帶的匿名使用者 ID 只拿來計算每分鐘的呼叫次數；Cloudflare 的防火牆會依連線的 IP 位址計算短時間內的請求次數；其他 ChatGPT 附帶的資料我們不使用。

12-3 保存期限

我們不保存工具參數、你的問題、位置或任何識別碼。連接器出錯時，程式只記一筆「哪個動作、什麼錯誤代碼」，Cloudflare 會加上時間等系統欄位；這筆紀錄不含參數或對話內容，存在 Cloudflare 帳號裡，最多保存 7 天後自動刪除。

12-4 誰會經手

  • Cloudflare：連接器的主機，經手所有連線，依 Cloudflare 隱私政策處理。回傳的牌圖網址指向 tarot.angelz13.com，載入牌圖時你的裝置或 AI 服務也會向 Cloudflare 送出一般連線資訊。
  • Anthropic／OpenAI：你在 Claude 或 ChatGPT 裡的對話，依 Anthropic 或 OpenAI 的隱私政策處理。

12-5 你可以怎麼控制

  • 在 Claude 或 ChatGPT 的連接器設定裡關掉或移除它；Claude 也可以在每段對話的「＋」›「連接器」單獨關掉。
  • 我們沒有保存你的資料，所以沒有可以申請刪除的紀錄；對話內容請向 Anthropic 或 OpenAI 申請刪除。

13. 內容免責聲明

ENGLISH VERSION

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026　・　Effective date: October 25, 2026

TL;DR: We collect no personal data. The 塔羅密語 (Tarot Whisper) iPhone app has no server: your draws and notes are stored on your iPhone and backed up automatically to your own iCloud, and content goes to OpenAI or ChatGPT only after you tap a button and agree. The website keeps no records at all. The connector's tools ask only for the parameters a draw needs, and neither ask for nor store your question; when called through ChatGPT, OpenAI also attaches data such as your approximate location (see section 12).

1. Who We Are

This policy covers the three products of 塔羅密語 (Tarot Whisper), all made by Alex Yeh:

  • 塔羅密語 iPhone app: shown as TAROT on devices set to English ("the app"). It is a tarot journal: draw a card, write down what is on your mind, and look back at how your mood has changed over time. The app has no backend server and no user accounts. Sections 2 to 10 are about the app.
  • 塔羅密語 website: tarot.angelz13.com, where you draw cards in your browser and get a prompt you can take to an AI. See section 11.
  • Tarot Whisper (塔羅密語 connector): an MCP connector (URL https://tarot.angelz13.com/mcp). Add it to your own Claude or ChatGPT to draw cards and look up card meanings in a conversation. See section 12 and the connector page.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

The app has no server, so we never receive or keep any of your data in the app, including:

  • Name, email, phone number, Apple ID, IDFA / IDFV, or any other identifier
  • Location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or health data (the app does not request these permissions)
  • Usage analytics (no Google Analytics, Firebase, Sentry, Crashlytics, or similar analytics or crash-reporting SDKs)
  • Advertising identifiers (no ad SDKs of any kind)

3. Data Stored on Your iPhone

The following data is stored on your device through iOS Capacitor Preferences and the Keychain, and only the app can read it:

  • Draw history: time, cards, upright or reversed, positions, mode, and the question, notes, mood and tags you enter; if you asked for an AI reading, the reading is saved too.
  • Your custom tags
  • OpenAI API key (only if you paste one in Settings): stored in the iOS Keychain and never included in the app's iCloud backup.
  • Settings: daily reminder, reading engine and model, and your consent choices for third-party AI.
  • Home Screen widget: today's card is shared with the widget through an iOS App Group, on this iPhone only.

4. Your Own iCloud Backup

When your draw history or custom tags change, the app automatically saves a copy to your own iCloud (in a folder that belongs to this app, at most one copy every 10 minutes, plus one extra copy before you clear your history). The backup lives in your Apple Account and is kept by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. We cannot see or access it.

To restore, tap "從 iCloud 還原" (Restore from iCloud) on the History page and pick a point in time. To delete all backups at once, go to iPhone Settings > Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Storage, find the app and delete its data.

5. On-Device AI

By default, AI readings are generated on your iPhone with Apple's Foundation Models framework, so your question, cards and history do not leave the device. This requires an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on.

6. Data Sent Only With Your Consent

For both destinations below, the first time you use them the app explains what will be sent and asks for your consent. Nothing is sent until you agree, and you can revoke each consent at any time in Settings › 第三方 AI 分享 (Third-party AI sharing). Revoking affects future use only; content already sent is handled under that provider's privacy policy, and deletion requests should go to them.

6-1 OpenAI (when AI readings use OpenAI)

If you choose OpenAI in Settings, or on-device AI is unavailable and you have set a key, the app connects to the OpenAI API directly from your iPhone with your own API key (no intermediary server) and sends:

  • The question, the drawn cards and their positions (for the choice spread, including the option names you entered)
  • Follow-up questions, and the whole conversation when you switch over from on-device AI
  • When you use Review on the History page, the entries and notes from that period

OpenAI handles this content under its privacy policy: openai.com/policies/privacy-policy/

6-2 ChatGPT on the web

When you tap "一鍵問 ChatGPT" or "詢問 ChatGPT" (Ask ChatGPT), the app opens ChatGPT on the web with the question, cards and positions; if you go there in the middle of a reading or conversation, the whole conversation (your follow-ups and the AI's answers) goes along. ChatGPT is run by OpenAI, and what you do there is handled under OpenAI's privacy policy. The app keeps no separate copy.

If you want nothing to leave your iPhone, use on-device AI only, do not set an OpenAI key, and do not tap the ChatGPT buttons. Drawing, history, Review, reminders and the widget all keep working.

7. Siri, Shortcuts, Journal and Reminders

  • Drawing with Siri: runs on your iPhone, and the card is saved to your local history.
  • Sending to Journal: the app places the entry and the spread image on the clipboard for a moment, hands them to a Shortcut you installed yourself to save into Apple's Journal app, and then clears the clipboard. The data stays on your iPhone and in Apple's services, under the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Daily reminders: local notifications scheduled on your iPhone, with no push server involved.

8. Children's Privacy

The app is not designed for children under 13 (or the equivalent age where you live) and does not knowingly collect personal data from children. Tarot content is spiritual and introspective, and the app is intended for adults.

9. Managing Your Data

  • Delete one entry: tap "刪除" (Delete) on that entry in the History page
  • Clear your OpenAI key: tap "清除" (Clear) in the OpenAI API Key section of Settings
  • Revoke third-party AI consent: in Settings › 第三方 AI 分享 (Third-party AI sharing)
  • Turn off daily reminders: use the reminder switch in Settings
  • Delete your iCloud backups: follow the steps in section 4
  • Delete all data on your iPhone: long-press the app icon on the Home Screen → Delete App. Note: this does not delete your iCloud backups; remove them separately as described in section 4.

10. Data Security

Your OpenAI API key is stored in the iOS Keychain through @aparajita/capacitor-secure-storage, protected by iOS hardware-backed encryption. Other data lives in the iOS sandbox assigned to the app, readable only by the app. All communication with the OpenAI API is encrypted with HTTPS.

11. The Website 塔羅密語

  • Nothing collected or stored: the website has no accounts, we set no cookies, and it runs no analytics or tracking code (Cloudflare's bot protection may set its own security cookie, __cf_bm; see "Fonts and hosting" below). Cards are drawn in your browser; your question and cards are not saved and are not sent to us. Reloading or closing the page clears them.
  • 複製 Prompt (Copy Prompt): the text is placed only on your device's clipboard.
  • 詢問 ChatGPT / 詢問 Claude (Ask ChatGPT / Ask Claude): tapping opens a new tab and carries the question, cards and positions in the web address to ChatGPT (run by OpenAI) or Claude (run by Anthropic). These two buttons do not show a separate confirmation. What you do there is handled under the OpenAI or Anthropic privacy policy.
  • Fonts and hosting: the website loads its font from Google Fonts and is hosted by Cloudflare, so your browser sends ordinary connection information (such as your IP address) to them, handled under the Google and Cloudflare privacy policies.

12. The Connector Tarot Whisper (塔羅密語)

Once you add the connector to your own Claude or ChatGPT, your AI connects to our program on Cloudflare whenever it decides to draw cards or look up a card. Your own AI does the reading, and your conversation stays in your own Claude or ChatGPT account. The connector has no accounts, its program sets no cookies, and it runs no analytics or tracking code; Cloudflare's bot protection may set its own security cookie (__cf_bm), handled under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

12-1 Data We Receive

  • Tool parameters: the draw mode (single, three cards, or daily) and whether to allow reversed cards; or the name and orientation of the card to look up. There is no field for your question, and extra fields are rejected.
  • Protocol information: technical fields such as the MCP protocol version and which tool is being called.
  • Data attached by ChatGPT: when called through ChatGPT, OpenAI attaches to each call an approximate location (city, region, country, time zone, latitude and longitude), locale, client information, an anonymous user ID, and conversation and organization identifiers. OpenAI decides whether to send these; we cannot turn them off.
  • Connection information: the connector is hosted by Cloudflare, so connections pass through Cloudflare with ordinary connection information such as IP addresses. Calls from Claude come from Anthropic's servers.

12-2 How We Use It

Only to draw cards, return card meanings, and limit how often the connector can be called in a short time to prevent abuse. The anonymous user ID attached by ChatGPT is used only to count calls per minute; Cloudflare's firewall counts requests per IP address over short periods; we do not use the other data ChatGPT attaches.

12-3 Retention

We do not store tool parameters, your question, your location, or any identifier. When the connector hits an error, the program logs only which action failed and the error code, and Cloudflare adds a timestamp and other system fields. This record contains no parameters or conversation content, is kept in our Cloudflare account, and is deleted automatically after at most 7 days.

12-4 Who Handles It

  • Cloudflare: hosts the connector and handles every connection, under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. The image URLs in the results point to tarot.angelz13.com, so loading a card image also sends ordinary connection information from your device or AI service to Cloudflare.
  • Anthropic / OpenAI: your conversations in Claude or ChatGPT are handled under the Anthropic or OpenAI privacy policy.

12-5 Your Choices

  • Turn the connector off or remove it in your Claude or ChatGPT connector settings; in Claude you can also turn it off for a single conversation from + › Connectors.
  • We keep none of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete; to delete conversations, contact Anthropic or OpenAI.
