塔羅密語連接器

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 25 日

一句話：「塔羅密語連接器」（目錄上的名稱是 Tarot Whisper）是一個 MCP 連接器。從 Claude 的連接器目錄或 ChatGPT 的外掛程式目錄把它加進來，在對話裡說「幫我抽三張牌」或「我抽到愚人逆位」，連接器負責抽牌、提供牌義，解讀由你自己的 AI 完成。免登入、不收費，不要求也不保存你的問題。

目前狀態：準備送上 Claude 連接器目錄與 ChatGPT 外掛程式目錄；上架後在目錄搜尋「Tarot Whisper」就找得到，這裡也會更新。

1. 它能做什麼

  • 抽牌：從 78 張偉特塔羅牌隨機抽牌，支援單張、過去／現在／未來三張牌陣、每日一張；可以選擇要不要有逆位。
  • 查牌義：你用自己的實體牌抽好了，告訴它牌名（中文或英文都可以）和正逆位，它回傳那張牌的資料。

每張牌會回傳：中英文牌名、牌組、元素、正位或逆位、關鍵字、象徵說明，以及牌圖網址。連接器只提供牌的資料，怎麼解讀、用什麼語氣，交給你自己的 AI。

2. 可以直接照打的範例

  • 幫我抽三張牌，看看這份工作接下來的發展。
  • 我今天用實體牌抽到愚人逆位，這張牌是什麼意思？
  • 幫我抽今天的每日一張，不要逆位。

3. 在 Claude 加入

  1. 到 claude.ai 或 Claude 桌面版的「自訂」›「連接器」（Customize › Connectors）。
  2. 選 Discover 瀏覽目錄，在搜尋框輸入「Tarot Whisper」，點開它的頁面。
  3. 按 Connect to Claude。這個連接器不需要登入，按下就連上。
  4. 回到連接器頁，「Tarot Whisper」會出現在 Your connectors，狀態是 Connected。

使用時，在對話框按「＋」›「連接器」（Connectors），確認「Tarot Whisper」的開關是開的。Claude 第一次要用它時可能會問你同不同意，選 Allow once（這次允許）或 Always allow（一律允許）。

Team、Enterprise 組織方案：組織還沒加這個連接器時，你會看到 Request（請求）按鈕或請你找組織擁有者（Owner）的提示；按 Request 會通知 Owner。Owner 看到的按鈕是 Connect for your team（替整個組織加入）；只想自己先試，可以按旁邊的箭頭選 Connect just for me。

出處：Anthropic 官方說明〈Get started with connectors〉〈Connectors directory〉。Claude 的畫面會改版，步驟以官方說明為準。

4. 在 ChatGPT 加入

  1. 到 ChatGPT 的「自訂」›「外掛程式」，或直接開 chatgpt.com/plugins。
  2. 在「公開」分頁的「搜尋外掛程式」輸入「Tarot Whisper」。
  3. 按旁邊的「＋」加入。
  4. 開新對話，在輸入框打 @ 選「Tarot Whisper」，再照上面的範例問。

出處：OpenAI 官方說明〈Upload and submit your plugin〉（上架後出現在外掛程式目錄）、〈Connect and test your plugin〉（用 @ 選外掛程式）；「自訂」「外掛程式」「公開」「搜尋外掛程式」這幾個介面名稱依 2026 年 10 月的實際畫面。ChatGPT 的畫面會改版，以實際畫面為準。

5. 你的資料

  • 工具只要求抽牌需要的參數（牌陣、要不要逆位，或你要查的牌名），沒有可以放問題文字的欄位；我們不保存任何參數。透過 ChatGPT 呼叫時，OpenAI 會另外附帶大概位置等資料。
  • 你和 AI 的對話、解讀，都留在你自己的 Claude 或 ChatGPT 帳號裡。
  • 完整說明（含 ChatGPT 每次呼叫會附帶哪些資料）見隱私政策第 12 節。

ENGLISH VERSION

Tarot Whisper Connector

Last updated: October 25, 2026

In short: Tarot Whisper (塔羅密語) is an MCP connector. Add it from the Claude Connectors Directory or the ChatGPT plugin directory, then say "Draw three cards for me" or "I drew The Fool reversed" in a conversation. The connector draws cards and provides card meanings; your own AI does the reading. No sign-in, no cost, and it neither asks for nor stores your question.

Status: being prepared for the Claude Connectors Directory and the ChatGPT plugin directory. Once listed, search for Tarot Whisper in either directory; this page will be updated.

1. What It Does

  • Draw cards: draws at random from the 78-card Rider–Waite–Smith deck, as a single card, a past / present / future three-card spread, or a daily card, with reversed cards on or off.
  • Look up a card: if you drew from your own physical deck, tell it the card's name (Chinese or English) and orientation, and it returns that card's data.

Each card comes back with its names in Chinese and English, arcana, element, upright or reversed orientation, keywords, symbolism notes, and an image URL. The connector provides card data only; how to read the cards, and in what tone, is up to your own AI.

2. Example Prompts

  • Draw three cards for me about where this job is heading.
  • I drew The Fool reversed from my own deck today. What does it mean?
  • Draw my daily card for today, with no reversed cards.

3. Add It in Claude

  1. In claude.ai or the Claude desktop app, go to Customize › Connectors.
  2. Select Discover to browse the directory, search for Tarot Whisper, and open its page.
  3. Select Connect to Claude. This connector needs no sign-in, so it connects right away.
  4. Back on the Connectors page, Tarot Whisper appears under Your connectors with the status Connected.

To use it, select + › Connectors in the message box and make sure its toggle is on. The first time Claude wants to use it, Claude may ask for your approval: choose Allow once or Always allow.

Team and Enterprise plans: if your organization has not added the connector yet, you see a Request button or a note to ask an Owner; Request notifies your Owners. Owners see Connect for your team, which adds it for everyone; to try it alone first, select the arrow next to it, then Connect just for me.

Source: Anthropic, "Get started with connectors" and "Connectors directory". Claude's screens change over time; follow the official pages if they differ.

4. Add It in ChatGPT

  1. In ChatGPT, open the Plugins page, or go to chatgpt.com/plugins.
  2. On the public tab, search for Tarot Whisper.
  3. Select the + button next to it.
  4. Start a new conversation, type @ to select Tarot Whisper, and try the examples above.

Source: OpenAI, "Upload and submit your plugin" (published plugins appear in the plugin directory) and "Connect and test your plugin" (selecting a plugin with @); the page and tab names follow the ChatGPT screens as of October 2026, which change over time.

5. Your Data

  • The tools ask only for what a draw needs (the spread, whether to allow reversed cards, or the name of the card to look up). There is no field for your question, and we store none of these parameters. When called through ChatGPT, OpenAI also attaches data such as your approximate location.
  • Your conversation and reading stay in your own Claude or ChatGPT account.
  • For full details, including the data ChatGPT attaches to each call, see section 12 of the Privacy Policy.
