支援與常見問題

最後更新：2026 年 10 月 14 日

這一頁是 iPhone App「塔羅手帳」的支援頁。同一個網址上的網站「塔羅密語」是另一個產品：在瀏覽器裡抽牌、產生可以帶去問 AI 的提示文字，不存任何紀錄；網站有問題也歡迎來信。

找不到答案？來信：alex.yeh@angelz13.com（通常 1-2 個工作天內回覆）

1. AI 解讀有哪幾種？

  • iPhone 本機 AI（預設）：直接在手機上產生，不用金鑰、不用付費、沒網路也能用，問題和牌不會離開手機。需要支援 Apple 智慧的 iPhone（iPhone 15 Pro 以上），並開啟 Apple 智慧。牌陣太長超過本機上限時，有設 OpenAI 金鑰會自動改用 OpenAI。
  • OpenAI：解讀比較細，要用你自己的 OpenAI API 金鑰（見第 2 題），每次會用到一點你自己的額度。在「⚙️ 設定 → 解讀引擎」切換。
  • 一鍵問 ChatGPT：抽完牌按這個，會開啟 ChatGPT 網頁並把問題和牌帶過去，不用金鑰。

用 OpenAI 或 ChatGPT 之前，App 會先說明會送出什麼、問你同不同意（見第 7 題）。

2. 如何取得 OpenAI API key？

只有想用 OpenAI 解讀才需要；用本機 AI 完全不用。首次設定約 5 分鐘、要綁信用卡：

  1. 到 platform.openai.com/signup 註冊 OpenAI 開發者帳號（跟一般 ChatGPT 帳號是不同入口）
  2. 登入後左側點 Billing → 加信用卡 → 首次儲值至少美金 5 元
  3. 左側點 API keys → Create new secret key → 命名「Tarot iOS」→ 複製整串（sk- 開頭；金鑰只會顯示一次）
  4. 回到 App 進「⚙️ 設定」貼上、按「儲存」，再按「測試連線」確認

金鑰存在 iPhone 的 Keychain，App 無法替你向 OpenAI 收費；用量在 platform.openai.com/usage 查得到。估計每次解讀：mini 約 NT$1-2、旗艦約 NT$3-5（依問題與牌數）。

3. AI 解讀沒回應或報錯怎麼辦？

  • 設定裡沒有本機 AI 可選：這支 iPhone 不支援 Apple 智慧，或還沒開啟 Apple 智慧。可以改用 OpenAI，或抽完按「一鍵問 ChatGPT」。
  • 連線錯誤／逾時：確認網路通暢，再試一次。
  • 401 Unauthorized：API key 錯誤或失效。回設定頁重貼一次，按「測試連線」確認。
  • 429 Too Many Requests：超過 OpenAI 的速率限制，等 1 分鐘再試。
  • 402／Insufficient quota：OpenAI 帳號餘額用完了，到 platform.openai.com 加值。
  • 5xx Server Error：OpenAI 那邊暫時異常，稍後再試。

4. 換手機或紀錄不見了，怎麼救回？

抽牌紀錄有變動時，App 會自動備份一份到你自己的 iCloud（最多 10 分鐘一份，清空紀錄前也會另外存一份）。要救回時：

  1. 確認新手機登入的是同一個 Apple 帳號、iCloud 有開
  2. 打開 App，到「📓 紀錄」頁按「從 iCloud 還原」
  3. 挑一個時間點還原

App 顯示「已備份」代表這支手機已經寫好，什麼時候上傳到 iCloud 由系統安排，沒網路時會晚一點。紀錄也可以匯出成 Markdown 自己保存。iCloud 備份不含 OpenAI 金鑰，換手機後要重新貼一次。

5. 怎麼用 Siri 抽牌？

不用先打開 App，直接對 Siri 說「用塔羅手帳抽一張牌」。Siri 會隨機抽一張、唸出牌名和正逆位，並打開 App 顯示這張牌，同時存進紀錄。「捷徑」App 裡也會自動列出這個動作。

6. 怎麼把紀錄送到 iPhone 的「日誌」App？

這要先裝一個捷徑。第一次用時，App 會顯示捷徑的安裝連結，照著加入就好。之後在紀錄上按送到日誌，那筆紀錄和牌陣圖會存進 Apple 的「日誌」App。

7. 怎麼撤回送到 OpenAI 或 ChatGPT 的同意？

到「⚙️ 設定 → 第三方 AI 分享」，逐一撤回。撤回之後，下次用到時 App 會再問你一次。

8. 我可以離線使用嗎？

抽牌、看牌義、紀錄、回顧、本機 AI 解讀、每日提醒、小工具都可以在沒網路時使用。

OpenAI 解讀和「一鍵問 ChatGPT」需要連線；iCloud 備份會在有網路時才上傳。

Support & FAQ

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This page supports the iPhone app 塔羅手帳 (Tarot Notebook). The website 塔羅密語 at the same address is a separate product: it draws cards in your browser and gives you a prompt to take to an AI, and keeps no records. Questions about the website are welcome by email too.

Can't find what you need? Email alex.yeh@angelz13.com (we usually reply within 1-2 business days).

1. What kinds of AI reading are there?

  • On-device AI (default): generated on your iPhone, with no key, no cost and no internet needed, and your question and cards never leave the device. Requires an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later) with Apple Intelligence turned on. If a spread is too long for the on-device limit and you have set an OpenAI key, the app switches to OpenAI.
  • OpenAI: more detailed readings using your own OpenAI API key (see question 2), which uses a little of your own credit each time. Switch engines in "⚙️ Settings → 解讀引擎 (Reading engine)".
  • 一鍵問 ChatGPT (Ask ChatGPT): tap it after drawing to open ChatGPT on the web with your question and cards. No key needed.

Before anything goes to OpenAI or ChatGPT, the app explains what will be sent and asks for your consent (see question 7).

2. How do I get an OpenAI API key?

You only need one for OpenAI readings; on-device AI needs no key. First-time setup takes about 5 minutes and requires a credit card:

  1. Sign up at platform.openai.com/signup (the developer portal, separate from ChatGPT)
  2. In the left sidebar, click Billing → add a credit card → top up at least USD $5
  3. Click API keys → Create new secret key → name it "Tarot iOS" → copy the full string (starts with sk-; it is shown only once)
  4. Back in the app, open "⚙️ Settings", paste the key, tap "儲存" (Save), then "測試連線" (Test connection)

The key is stored in your iPhone's Keychain, and the app cannot charge you through OpenAI; you can check usage at platform.openai.com/usage. Each reading costs roughly NT$1-2 with a mini model and NT$3-5 with a flagship model, depending on the question and number of cards.

3. AI reading not responding or showing errors?

  • No on-device AI option in Settings: this iPhone does not support Apple Intelligence, or Apple Intelligence is turned off. Use OpenAI instead, or tap "一鍵問 ChatGPT" after drawing.
  • Connection error / timeout: check your network and try again.
  • 401 Unauthorized: invalid or expired API key. Paste it again in Settings and tap "Test connection".
  • 429 Too Many Requests: OpenAI rate limit exceeded. Wait a minute and try again.
  • 402 / Insufficient quota: your OpenAI balance has run out. Top up at platform.openai.com.
  • 5xx Server Error: a temporary problem on OpenAI's side. Try again later.

4. New phone, or lost my history?

When your history changes, the app automatically backs it up to your own iCloud (at most one copy every 10 minutes, plus an extra copy before you clear your history). To get it back:

  1. Make sure the new iPhone is signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on
  2. Open the app, go to the "📓 History" page and tap "從 iCloud 還原" (Restore from iCloud)
  3. Pick a point in time to restore

"已備份" (Backed up) means the copy has been written on this iPhone; iOS decides when it uploads to iCloud, so it may take longer without a connection. You can also export your history as Markdown. iCloud backups do not include your OpenAI key, so paste it again on a new phone.

5. How do I draw with Siri?

Without opening the app, say "用塔羅手帳抽一張牌" to Siri (Siri phrases are in Chinese). Siri draws a random card, reads its name and whether it is upright or reversed, opens the app to show it, and saves it to your history. The same action is listed in the Shortcuts app.

6. How do I send an entry to Apple's Journal app?

This needs a Shortcut. The first time, the app shows the install link; add the Shortcut from there. After that, send an entry from your history and it is saved, with its spread image, into Apple's Journal app.

7. How do I revoke consent for OpenAI or ChatGPT?

Go to "⚙️ Settings → 第三方 AI 分享 (Third-party AI sharing)" and revoke each one. After you revoke, the app will ask again the next time.

8. Can I use the app offline?

Drawing, card meanings, history, Review, on-device AI readings, daily reminders and the widget all work offline.

OpenAI readings and "一鍵問 ChatGPT" need a connection, and iCloud backups upload once you are online.
